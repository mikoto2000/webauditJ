03 Response options and approvals

After reading this chapter: Follow the steps below to assign an owner, the next tasks and a reporting date.

Recommended approach for this investigation

Proceed with targeted investigation, starting with priority cases

Section 03 sets the overall investigation approach. Select targets in section 02 and use section 04 to assign owners, records and deadlines per target. Decide individual restrictions or shutdowns based on the target evidence and operational impact.

Steps to carry out this approach

  1. Contact system administration or security and appoint an investigator and a responsible decision owner.
  2. Ask the investigator to preserve access logs and related records for the relevant period before they are overwritten or deleted. Section 04 lists records to seek.
  3. Decide the investigation scope and ask the investigator to start with high-priority cases in section 02, correlating the records in section 04 to assess whether harm occurred.
  4. Set an initial reporting deadline and request findings on harm, affected scope, unresolved questions and the next required response.

05 Decision conditions, closure and limitations

After reading this chapter: Use this chapter after receiving the results of the additional investigation in section 04. Based on those results, decide whether to strengthen the response, continue investigating or consider closure. Record what could not be established and do not conclude safety while questions remain unresolved.

Decision rules common to all targets

If additional records establish unauthorized activity or continuing or expanding harm, the responsible owner reviews the current situation and operational impact and decides whether to strengthen the response. Access-log detections alone do not automatically authorize restrictions or shutdowns.

If investigation is deferred: Deferring investigation leaves harm unresolved and logs may be overwritten, preventing later verification.

Additional escalation criteria by case type

These criteria apply if corroborating evidence emerges during further investigation; they are not findings of confirmed harm. Targets sharing the same criteria are grouped.

Sensitive resource exposure

If historical response content or file-access records establish that non-public file content was returned or read, investigate the exposed information and affected scope and consider restricting the affected route.

Targets: WA-68533c54f477aefb WA-53546a87d8946592 DET-3f3155956d35ab191969098eb3f28b05 DET-f46f1e0d8936861b6d6ee668e0da5ee5

Unintended processing or information access

If application, database, host or browser evidence establishes unintended processing, data access or modification, or script execution caused by the input, investigate affected operations and data and consider function restrictions or detailed investigation.

Targets: WA-e209a83ef4765316 WA-e3ae05f55589ce85 DET-e04a802150d1b00d86916f0c97ab9cc8

Conditions for considering closure

  • Record priority-case results and reasons for unresolved outcomes.
  • Record interim/preventive reviews and unevaluated scope.
  • A responsible owner decides continued response or residual-risk acceptance; no automatic closure.

Decision limits

Analysis of supplied inputs completed; complete collection of all access is not established. Count quality: exact · Full request evidence complete: true

  • Bodies and authentication/operation audits are unavailable; internal execution, disclosure and unauthorized logins cannot be confirmed.
  • Parsing supplied logs does not establish complete collection or investigation of all access.
  • Current exposure, asset criticality, losses, cost, actual owners and deadlines cannot be determined from access logs.
  • Engine detections, cases and requests overlap; do not sum them as unique attacks.
Response-level criteria
urgentA responsible owner considers urgent response after corroborating disclosure, unauthorized critical operations or current severe impairment; not selected automatically from access logs.
priorityA possible-success case or a case with high investigation priority exists.
holdNo priority evidence and either zero parsed observations or incomplete analysis.
plannedReview candidates exist without high priority, including unassessed independent detections.
monitorNo candidates in the completed evaluated scope; not a guarantee of security.

Tertiary investigation: if outcomes or scope remain unresolved, identify the required host/database/forensic evidence and objectives and seek the responsible owner's decision.
